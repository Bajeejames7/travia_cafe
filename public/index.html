FAQ

Common questions

How do I pay?

After you enrol, your dashboard shows the exact amount and your reference. You pay from your phone with M-PESA:

    Stay safe. Only pay to the number shown on this site and check the name in the M-PESA prompt before entering your PIN. We will never call to ask for your PIN or ask you to "reverse" a payment.

    What happens after I pay?

    Self-paced videos appear in your dashboard as soon as your payment is confirmed, and play on any phone, tablet or computer. For live and in-person classes we email you a one-time code, which is your ticket, and live class links show up in your dashboard.

    How long does it take to confirm my payment?

    We check each M-PESA payment and confirm it, usually quickly during working hours. You get an email as soon as it's done.

    Can I sign in with Google?

    Yes. Choose "Continue with Google" on the log-in page. Signing in only opens your account: a course still opens once its payment has been confirmed, the same as with an email and password.

    Can I share my account?

    No. Your account can be signed in on up to 2 devices at a time, and signing in on another device signs out the oldest one.
