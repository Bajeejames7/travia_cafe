    FAQ

    Common questions

    How do I get my course after paying?

    Self-paced videos appear in your dashboard as soon as your payment is confirmed. For live and in-person classes we email you a one-time code, which is your ticket, and live class links show up in your dashboard.

    How long does it take to confirm my payment?

    We check each M-PESA payment and confirm it, usually quickly during working hours. You get an email as soon as it's done.

    Can I sign in with Google?

    Yes. Choose "Continue with Google" on the log-in page. Signing in only opens your account: a course still opens once its payment has been confirmed, the same as with an email and password.

    Can I watch the videos on my phone?

    Yes. Videos play in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to install.

    Can I share my account with a friend?

    No. Your account can be signed in on up to 2 devices at a time, and signing in on another device signs out the oldest one.

    I forgot my password. What do I do?

    On the log-in page, choose "Forgot your password?" and we'll email you a reset link. It works for one hour.
